# Word lists

`profanity-da.txt`, `profanity-de.txt` and `profanity-en.txt` are the
`foul_language.voc` files of
[ovos-good-manners-pipeline-plugin](https://github.com/OpenVoiceOS/ovos-good-manners-pipeline-plugin)
(`locale/da-dk`, `de-de`, `en-us`), Apache License 2.0, by Andreas Lorensen
and the OpenVoiceOS contributors.

Copied from commit `6cdbfb7ccdc3712a2113b1afd6aeb4a7ed59a979` (27 Oct 2026),
unchanged. One word or phrase per line, lower case.

To fetch newer ones: `contrib/update-wordlists.sh`. Words that turn out wrong
for text (they were made for speech) are better fixed upstream, so OVOS gains
too.
